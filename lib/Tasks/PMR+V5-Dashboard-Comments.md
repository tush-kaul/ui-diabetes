Diabetic foot

-   Duration of diabetes, at least 1 year, irrespective of HbA1c control, 1st time screen, if no symptoms, every year once annual screening
-   Alert positive to both medicine and PMR specialists
-   Symptom screening for referral on screen positives (Need to add few to V6 screening document, if not already added)
-   h/o tingling, numbness, parasthesia
-   h/o PVD (intermittent pain on walking)
-   h/o not able to grip chappals
-   h/o obvious sensory loss- not able to differentiate hot and cold/ not able to sense feel of wearing sox
-   h/o obvious foot deformity- e.g. big toe deviation, toe curling
-   h/o skin changes- hyper pigmentation, fissures, corns, callosities
-   h/o foot ulcer- if positive- details on when, where, time taken to heal
-   h/o balance issue- when the feet is on ground, not able to hold balance
-   h/o prior amputation- e.g. toe amputation / any amputation
-   h/o lower limb joint pain (hip, knee)/ osteoarthritis with or without deformities
